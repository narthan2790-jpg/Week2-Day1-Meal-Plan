# Reusable Prompt: 7-Day Vegetarian South Indian Meal Plan

## EDITABLE FOR BLOCK

- Person's age: 42
- Goal: Maintain weight and improve energy
- Activity level: Moderately active
- Food preference: Vegetarian
- Allergies: None
- Foods disliked or avoided: Brinjal
- Cooking time on weekdays: 20 minutes
- Number of people eating: 2
- Other practical notes: Budget-friendly Tamil Nadu meals, suitable for a working couple.

## ROLE
You are a practical meal-planning assistant familiar with everyday South Indian vegetarian home cooking. Create a realistic, balanced meal plan using familiar dishes and ingredients commonly available in Tamil Nadu and other parts of South India.

## TASK
Create a healthy 7-day vegetarian South Indian meal plan for the person described in the EDITABLE FOR BLOCK, supporting gradual, sustainable weight loss.

## CONSTRAINTS
1. Use vegetarian South Indian dishes and name specific, recognisable dishes.
2. Include all four meals for every day: breakfast, lunch, one snack and dinner.
3. Give ordinary, practical portion sizes for every meal.
4. Aim for balanced meals with vegetables, pulses/legumes, whole grains and suitable vegetarian protein sources.
5. Use ingredients commonly available locally and practical for home cooking.
6. Keep weekday preparation realistic within the cooking time in the FOR block.
7. Include variety across seven days; avoid repeating the same main dish too often.
8. Respect all allergies and dislikes. Never include a listed allergen, even as an optional topping.
9. Do not prescribe extreme calorie restriction, fasting, meal skipping, detoxes or unrealistic weight-loss promises.
10. Do not assume medical conditions or invent personal health details. For medical or special dietary needs, advise confirmation with a qualified dietitian or healthcare professional.
11. Do not make quinoa, avocado or imported specialty foods the foundation of this South Indian plan.
12. If a detail cannot be determined from the information provided, state the assumption briefly.

## OUTPUT FORMAT
Return exactly ONE continuous Markdown table.

Use this exact structure:

| Day | Meal | Dish | Portion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Breakfast | Specific dish | Exact portion |
| Monday | Lunch | Specific dish | Exact portion |

Requirements:
1. Include exactly 28 data rows.
2. Include Monday through Sunday in order.
3. Include Breakfast, Lunch, Snack and Dinner for each day.
4. Use exactly four columns.
5. Do not create separate tables for different days.
6. Do not put blank lines between table rows.
7. Do not put each cell in a separate table or paragraph.
8. Do not add extra columns.
9. After the table, add a short section titled Practical notes.
10. Ensure the final response displays as one properly formatted Markdown table.
11. Give all portion quantities for the total of two people, not per person.
12. Output the Markdown table as plain text with one header row,
one separator row and 28 consecutive data rows.

## AVOID
- Vague descriptions such as “healthy breakfast”; name the actual dish.
- Non-vegetarian foods, eggs and listed allergens.
- Unrealistic portions or expensive, hard-to-find ingredients as routine items.
- Shaming language, guaranteed results or claims of rapid weight loss.

Now create the 7-day plan using the FOR block above. Follow the requested table format exactly.
